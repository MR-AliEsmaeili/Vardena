## State/ownership contract

`accountingEligible`, requested, draft-created, draft-cancelled, failed/refused and `accountingPosted` are distinct. Draft creation **never** sets Accounting Posted; only Accounting finalization establishes final voucher/GL effect. Draft cancellation returns the Inventory document to its applicable normal lifecycle without erasing audit evidence. In direct mode a final voucher/reference is visible on Inventory; in draft mode Inventory shows draft status/reference, later final status/reference or cancellation. Inventory headers of Accounting-capable documents must communicate state and appropriate draft/final number; exact Persian UI copy and frontend work are downstream. Accounting owns draft, operation, voucher and journal, including Inventory draft category; Inventory must call an Accounting public application contract, not write Accounting tables.

A same-Legal-Entity warehouse transfer creates no GL voucher (PO-06). Cost-equal Purchase Return may post, but agreed-price variance remains blocked under OQ-70. Sales Return Accounting remains deferred (PO-12). No correction, reversal, compensating journal, cross-entity transfer accounting, worker posting or frontend is authorized by this record.
